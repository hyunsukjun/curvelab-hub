Playback and saved audio

Space plays or pauses outside inputs, selectors and help. Switching Labs saves the processed working audio and current Lab settings as a new stage. With a selected stage focused, Delete removes that stage and selects its predecessor. With focus in history, Command+Z restores the last deleted stage. Shift+Command+Z deletes it again. Download WAV exports the selected saved stage. Edits made after that stage was saved are included when you switch Labs.

Processing and history

The waveform shows the working audio. Source edits have their own undo and redo, and selecting a different stage starts from its saved audio. Choose Original in Edit Audio to restore the original for that source. Each Lab uses its existing live playback and file-rendering path; Space headphone preview and WAV can differ. Each stage retains its existing output fades and protection.

History is memory-only, up to 30 stages / 256 MiB audio. Closing or reloading loses the session.

Files and limits

  • Input: up to 3 minutes. Mono is copied equally to Left and Right; multichannel input is not accepted.
  • WAV, MP3 and other browser-supported audio. Long or memory-heavy files may fail to decode; the previous valid audio is retained.
  • AIFF: mono/stereo 16/24-bit PCM, 8–192 kHz. Compressed AIFF-C and unsupported AIFF variants cannot be opened.
  • AIFF limits: 128 MiB file / converted WAV; 192 MiB decoded audio. Other formats depend on browser codecs and available memory.
  • Working audio: 48 kHz stereo. Download: 24-bit WAV. Closing or reloading loses this memory-only session.
